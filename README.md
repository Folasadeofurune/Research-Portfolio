**Current Research:** 
Mental Health Among Black Immigrants in Canada

My doctoral research examined the relationship between age at immigration, gender, and self-reported mood and anxiety disorders among Black immigrants in Canada using nationally representative survey data. This work contributes to understanding how migration experiences shape mental health outcomes and identifies opportunities for improving culturally responsive public health policies and services. 

**Research Philosophy**
I believe that high-quality research should do more than generate knowledge. It should support better decisions, improve public policies, strengthen health systems, and contribute to measurable improvements in people's lives. My work emphasizes rigorous methods, transparent reporting, and practical application of research findings.
